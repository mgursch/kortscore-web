When the match ends, the app saves the workout to Apple Health. The heart rate and energy readings your watch recorded during the workout are stored with it by Apple Health itself.

iPhone: The iPhone app asks for Apple Health permission only so it can open Kortscore on your Apple Watch when you start a match from your iPhone. It does not read or write any health data.

Withdrawing permission: You can revoke access at any time: for Apple Health on your iPhone under Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Kortscore, on Wear OS under Settings → Apps → Kortscore → Permissions. You can delete workouts saved by Kortscore in the Health app at any time.
