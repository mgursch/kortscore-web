For clubs

Put up the flyer.
Members save.

Your club puts up the Kortscore flyer in the clubhouse or at the courts, and your members get a discount code for Kortscore Plus in return. Just drop us a line, we sort out the rest by email.

How it works

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Send us a short email with your club name, town and a contact person. The button below fills it in for you.

  2. 02

    Put up the flyer

    Print the flyer on A4 and put it up where people see it: in the clubhouse, on the notice board or at the entrance to the courts.

  3. 03

    Pass on the code

    You get your own code for Kortscore Plus and pass it on to your members, on the notice board, in the newsletter or the club group chat. It makes Plus cheaper in the app.

How your members redeem the code

  1. Open Kortscore and go to the Kortscore Plus purchase screen, for example in the settings under Unlock Kortscore Plus.
  2. Tap Redeem code and enter your club’s code.
  3. The lower price appears right on the purchase screen. The purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play as usual.

It works the same on iPhone and Android.

What is Kortscore Plus?

Kortscore itself is free: scoring on the wrist, match history and the live ticker, with no ads and no account. Kortscore Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and adds advanced statistics, automatic backups and the AI coach that analyses matches. With your code, your members get Plus for less.

The flyer

A4, one page, with a QR code to download the app. The flyer is in German for now. Print it in colour if you can, the screens and the watch come across much better.

Questions

Does it cost the club anything?

No. You put up the flyer, we send the discount code. Kortscore itself is free for your members anyway.

How big is the discount?

We agree on that with each club individually. Tell us roughly how many members you have and we will make you an offer.

Does the code work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The code is redeemed in the Kortscore app on the Kortscore Plus purchase screen, the same way on both platforms.

Does the code expire?

It can. A code may have a validity period, and we will tell you along with the code.
