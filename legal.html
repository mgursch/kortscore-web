Privacy policy

This policy applies to the website kortscore.com and to the Kortscore app (Wear OS and Android). It describes which data we process and why.

The most important point first

Your match data stays on your devices as long as you do not share a match live. Without this feature we transmit no scores, statistics or match histories to us or to third parties. There is no user account and no registration. If you deliberately share a match via a link, exactly that match is uploaded to a server – see Live sharing via link.

Kortscore app

Match data stays on your devices

Scores, match history, statistics and the archive are stored exclusively locally on the watch and the phone. The transfer between watch and phone runs via the Wear OS pairing (Bluetooth or local network) and not via our servers. We have no access to this data. A user account is not required.

This applies as long as you do not share a match live. Live sharing is the only feature in which match data leaves your device. It is off by default and must be started separately for each match.

Live sharing via link

You can share an ongoing or finished match via a link so that others can follow the score. This feature is voluntary and must be activated separately for each match. Without your explicit activation no match leaves your device; nothing else about the app's behaviour changes.

When you activate it, the app asks for two display names ("Your name" and "Opponent"), which you type in yourself. The dialog points out that these names are publicly visible. So you decide for yourself whether you use real names, first names or nicknames.

What is transmitted

  • the two display names you entered
  • the complete point log of the match (who won which point and when, with timestamps)
  • the match configuration (set format, no-ad and the like), the first server, start and end time as well as the match status (in progress, finished or abandoned)

Not transmitted are your email address, location, device ID or your real name – the latter only if you type it in as a display name yourself.

The link is unlisted, but public

The link takes the form share.kortscore.com/<ID> with a 22-character, cryptographically random ID that cannot be guessed. The page is excluded from search engines via noindex, and the database does not allow matches to be listed, only retrieved via the exact ID. Anyone who knows the link, however, can view the score without signing in and pass the link on. Treat it therefore like a publication within a small circle, not like a password.

Spectators need neither the app nor an account. The page reads only that one shared match, sets no cookies and contains no reach measurement.

Recipients and storage location

For live sharing we use Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): Cloud Firestore as the database and Firebase Hosting for the page share.kortscore.com. The database is located in the EU region (europe-west). Like any web server, Firebase Hosting logs the IP addresses of requests for technical reasons; in that respect Google is the operator. Details: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The first time you share, the app creates an anonymous Firebase sign-in: a random identifier tied to the installation, without a login and without any input from you. It is not contained in the publicly readable match but in a separate record that nobody can read, and its sole purpose is to ensure that only your device can write to your link. If you never share, this service is never contacted either. To protect against misuse we additionally use Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity.

Duration and deactivation

The link does not expire automatically. The shared match remains stored until you select "Deactivate link" in the app. Deactivation is final and is enforced server-side: afterwards the page is no longer accessible to anyone, not even to people who already have the link. The record is not, however, physically deleted in the process, but kept in the database in a blocked state. We carry out the final deletion on request by email to swordistudios@gmail.com.

As long as a match is being shared live, the app shows a persistent notification with an action to stop it, so that sharing does not continue unnoticed.

The legal basis is the performance of the service you requested, namely providing the link (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and, for publishing the display names you entered, your consent, which you give by activating sharing (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by deactivating the link in the app.

Usage statistics and stability

The phone app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) in order to understand which features are used and to detect and fix errors.

Only coarse, non-personal information is deliberately reported:

  • a match was archived (singles or doubles, number of sets, playing time as a range such as "30–60 minutes")
  • a match was shared (singles or doubles, number of sets) – without stating whether as an image or via link, and without the link ID
  • a backup was created or restored (number of matches it contains)
  • whether a watch was connected at the time of a crash

Neither scores nor names, opponents, times of individual matches or any other content are transmitted. In addition, Firebase processes technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, approximate region and a randomly generated installation ID, as well as crash data in the event of an error. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a functioning and stable app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Backups

Backups of your matches are written to a folder that you choose yourself. The file only leaves your device if you choose a folder that is synced with a cloud service. In that case the terms of the respective provider additionally apply.

Sharing as an image

Independently of live sharing, the app can create an image of the match and hand it over to the application you select in the share dialog. Nothing is transmitted to us in the process; what happens to the image afterwards depends on the respective application.

Permissions

The app only requests vibration (haptic feedback), notifications and a foreground service so that an ongoing match keeps counting when the display is locked. The watch app requests no internet permission.

Installation via Google Play

The app is obtained and installed via Google Play. We have no influence over the data processed by Google in the process. Details: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your rights

You have the rights against us to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing based on a legitimate interest (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). An informal email to swordistudios@gmail.com is sufficient.

We have no access to the match data on your devices, so we can neither hand it over nor delete it. You can delete it yourself in the app at any time or uninstall the app.

Matches that you have shared via a link are a different case: these are stored on our server. You can deactivate the link yourself in the app at any time, which makes the match inaccessible to everyone. We carry out the final deletion of the record on request – an email with the link in question is sufficient for that.

If you believe that the processing of your data infringes the GDPR, you can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at).
