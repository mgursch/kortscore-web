The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure and efficient operation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Analytics (Umami)

We use Umami (Umami Software, Inc.) to measure reach. Umami works without cookies and without recognising individual people. The data recorded is aggregated: the page requested, the referrer, approximate origin at country level, device type, browser and operating system. IP addresses are not stored, no profile is created and no fingerprinting is used.

Because no personal data is stored in the process and no information is placed on or read from your device, no consent is required for this. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in data-minimising reach measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: umami.is/privacy.

All fonts are loaded from our own server. There is no connection to Google Fonts or other third-party providers.

Chat (Tawk.to)

For questions we offer a chat by Tawk.to (tawk.to inc., 187 East Warm Springs Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89119, USA). The chat is only loaded once you click the chat button. Until then there is no connection to Tawk.to and no cookies are set – anyone who does not chat never comes into contact with the service.

If you click the button, your browser loads the chat widget from Tawk.to. In doing so, your IP address, browser and device details as well as the page requested are transmitted to Tawk.to, and cookies are set that associate the chat history with your visit. We process the content you write in the chat in order to answer your enquiry. Please do not enter any sensitive data there.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by clicking the chat button (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for handling your enquiry additionally Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. The transfer to the USA is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can withdraw your consent at any time by no longer using the chat and deleting the cookies in your browser. Details: tawk.to/privacy-policy.

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to handle your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete enquiries as soon as they have been dealt with and no retention obligations stand in the way.
